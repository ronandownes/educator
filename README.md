# Ronan Downes — Education

A document-style professional notebook for teaching practice, subject knowledge, school research and interview preparation.

## Main structure

The site uses eight core interview domains plus two practical top-level shelves:

- Teaching & Learning
- Classes
- Classroom Management
- SEN / AEN & Inclusion
- Differentiation & Accessibility
- Assessment, Feedback & Reporting
- Planning & Curriculum
- Plans
- Relationships & Wellbeing
- Professional Responsibility & School Community
- Glossary
- Timeline

The logo is the Home link. There are no separate Home, Practice or Interview navigation tabs. **Classes** holds class profiles. **Plans** holds actual schemes of work and the curriculum specifications they use. Planning and curriculum interview questions remain under **Planning & Curriculum**.

Each interview-domain dropdown surfaces the typical questions for that area so the navigation acts as an interview-recall scaffold. Operational material is kept in Classes and Plans so users do not have to discover it inside question menus.

## Editing

The working content lives in Markdown files under `content/`. The repository includes a `.pages.yml` configuration for browser-based rich-text editing with Pages CMS.

See `AGENTS.md` for the durable information architecture, content-placement rules and editing guidance for future AI-assisted changes.

## Publishing

GitHub Pages publishes from `main` / root. The site is marked `noindex,nofollow` while it is a working draft. Do not add student names or confidential student information to the published repository.
