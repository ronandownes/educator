## Notes

If you mean **St Patrick’s Comprehensive School in Shannon**, I can’t find a separately stated formal motto on the school’s current official site.

The closest thing to their core public statement is:

**“Committed to excellence. We provide equality of opportunity for all students in a disciplined and caring environment.”** ([ST. PATRICK'S COMPREHENSIVE SCHOOL](https://www.stpatrickscomprehensive.ie/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "ST. PATRICK'S COMPREHENSIVE SCHOOL - Home"))

For interview prep, I’d compress that to:

**Excellence · Equality of Opportunity · Discipline · Care**

That is much more safely attributable to St Patrick’s than **“Hope. Expectation. Trust.”**, which is our classroom-management phrase.
