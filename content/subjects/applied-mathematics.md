## Applied Mathematics
I teach the subject as modelling: identify the system, make assumptions explicit, represent it mathematically, solve and then interpret the result in the physical context.

## Connection to my wider teaching
Applied Mathematics sits naturally between my Mathematics, Physics and modelling background.
