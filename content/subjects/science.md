## Junior Cycle Science
I use practical investigation, questioning, modelling and explicit teaching of scientific language so students can connect evidence to explanation.

## Inclusion in Science
Clear procedures, visual supports, structured practical roles and careful group organisation help make laboratory learning accessible and safe.
