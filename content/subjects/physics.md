## How I teach Physics
I connect mathematical representation, physical intuition, practical investigation and clear explanation. Students should be able to move between a phenomenon, a diagram, a graph, an equation and an interpretation.

## Practical work
Practical activity should produce evidence and reasoning, not simply a completed procedure.
