## Two-minute opening
I am a post-primary **Mathematics, Science, Physics, Computer Science and Applied Mathematics teacher** with substantial experience across Junior Cycle, Senior Cycle, LCA and learning support. A consistent thread through my work is making demanding subject content accessible without lowering expectations. I have taught mixed-ability classes, worked in learning-support and withdrawal settings, used technology and concrete materials, and collaborated with SEN/AEN colleagues and wider school-support structures.

What attracts me to St Patrick's is the fit between that experience and a school whose history is built around **comprehensive education and equality of opportunity**. For me, good teaching comes back to three things: **hope, expectation and trust**.

## What do you know about the Comp?
Established in Shannon in **1966 as Ireland's first comprehensive school**. Its mission combines **excellence, equality of opportunity, a disciplined and caring environment, individual potential and community**. I also see a strong present-day inclusion strand through AEN, Inbhear, L2LP, LCA, mixed-ability provision and the wider student-support system.

## Classroom management
**Hope · Expectation · Trust.** Prevent first through routines, clarity and relationships. Intervene calmly and proportionately. Restore the relationship and learning. Record persistent patterns. Collaborate with the correct staff. Escalate when the issue requires it.

## SEN / AEN
Start with the learner, not the label. Use support information. Work with AEN staff/SNAs. Adjust access through scaffolding, concrete and visual materials, technology, pace and task design. Keep the core learning and ambition visible. Build independence.

## Why St Patrick's?
The role combines two strands of my experience that belong together: **strong subject teaching and inclusive practice**. The comprehensive-school tradition is also meaningful to me because it is about breadth of opportunity rather than a narrow route through education.
