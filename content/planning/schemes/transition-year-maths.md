## Core aims

- repair important gaps without labelling the year as remediation
- extend reasoning and mathematical communication
- show mathematics in technology, design, finance, data and decision-making
- build readiness for senior cycle while preserving TY's exploratory character

## Suggested modules

| Module | Possible work | Product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data in the real world | Surveys, sampling, misleading graphs, spreadsheets | Short evidence-based report |
| Personal finance | Payslips, tax, budgeting, interest and comparison shopping | Financial decision brief |
| Mathematical modelling | Travel, sport, environment or school systems | Model with assumptions and limitations |
| Geometry and design | Scale, construction, optimisation, CAD or GeoGebra | Design portfolio |
| Coding and computation | Algorithms, variables, simulation and data | Small program or interactive model |
| Senior-cycle bridge | Algebra, functions and problem-solving habits | Individual readiness plan |

## Mixed-attainment and streamed settings

A mixed-attainment TY class benefits from choice of context, flexible roles and common outcomes with different levels of mathematical sophistication. If a school streams TY, each group should still receive worthwhile investigation, technology and reasoning—not simply a faster or slower textbook route.
