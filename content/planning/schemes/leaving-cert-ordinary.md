## Why a separate scheme

Ordinary Level requires its own pace and sequencing. It should not be a slowed-down copy of Higher Level, nor should students receive only isolated procedures. The plan prioritises secure foundations, intelligible representations, frequent success and enough cumulative practice for durable learning.

## Fifth Year

| Period | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Aug–Oct | Number, algebraic foundations, equations and functions |
| Nov–Dec | Coordinate geometry, graphs and interpretation |
| Jan–Feb | Geometry, trigonometry and measurement |
| Mar–Apr | Statistics, probability and data literacy |
| May | Cumulative consolidation and diagnostic review |

## Sixth Year

| Period | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Aug–Oct | Functions, calculus foundations and connected algebra |
| Nov–Dec | Financial mathematics, sequences and applications |
| Jan–Feb | Complete remaining content; mixed problem sets |
| Mar–Apr | Paper-based integration, targeted reteaching and timed practice |
| May | Final retrieval, confidence and examination routines |

## Non-negotiables

- diagnostic retrieval before each new sequence
- explicit links among words, diagrams, tables, graphs and symbols
- calculator fluency without surrendering estimation or checking
- short feedback cycles and fresh reassessment
- planned challenge for students who may move level
