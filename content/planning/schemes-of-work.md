## Planning principles

These are working schemes, not a promise to march through a textbook. Each one:

- begins with the specification and the students' actual starting points
- develops the unifying practices through Number, Geometry and trigonometry, Algebra and functions, and Statistics and probability
- includes retrieval, formative assessment, consolidation and planned flexibility
- distinguishes core entitlement from extension and additional challenge
- leaves space to reteach when evidence says the class is not ready to move on

The schemes are editable. Timing is indicative and should change when assessment evidence, school calendars or the class profile require it.
