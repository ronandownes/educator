> **Synthetic-data notice:** This class is entirely imaginary. The numbers and needs below are invented for planning and interview practice and do not describe real pupils.

## Class context

18 students in the more advanced of two TY Mathematics groups; the school uses prior attainment to stream for the year.

## Strengths and interests

Strong algebraic fluency, interest in STEM courses and willingness to persist with difficult tasks.

## Fictional learning and access profile

1 student with dyslexia; 2 with ADHD; 3 with high performance anxiety. Some students equate speed with intelligence and avoid visible mistakes.

## Starting evidence

High scores coexist with reluctance to conjecture, explain or revise a failed approach.

## Planned teaching response

Use modelling, coding, proof and data projects where uncertainty is normal. Make reasoning, revision and collaboration visible assessment criteria. Coordinate with the other TY group so streaming does not narrow entitlement.

## Assessment and review loop

Investigation journals, model critique, peer explanation and a final presentation with individual mathematical reflection.
