> **Synthetic-data notice:** This class is entirely imaginary. The numbers and needs below are invented for planning and interview practice and do not describe real pupils.

## Class context

22 students preparing for the Junior Cycle examination while completing remaining learning outcomes.

## Strengths and interests

Reliable effort, improving calculator use and strong response to clear success criteria.

## Fictional learning and access profile

2 students with dyslexia; 2 with ADHD; 1 student with significant school-avoidance history; 7 with fragile algebra and proportional reasoning.

## Starting evidence

Past-paper marks fluctuate because students recognise topics in isolation but struggle to choose a method in mixed questions.

## Planned teaching response

Interleave topics early, teach strategy selection explicitly, analyse errors without shame and use short reteaching cycles. Preserve conceptual explanations alongside examination technique.

## Assessment and review loop

Weekly mixed retrieval, half-term common assessments, error coding and reassessment after targeted repair.
