> **Synthetic-data notice:** This class is entirely imaginary. The numbers and needs below are invented for planning and interview practice and do not describe real pupils.

## Class context

5 students attend two planned support sessions each week. This is a non-SEN AEN profile centred on access, regulation, attendance and reconnection with learning.

## Strengths and interests

Creative interests, humour, strong relationships with selected adults and good engagement when the purpose and endpoint are clear.

## Fictional learning and access profile

Fictional needs include anxiety, disrupted attendance, bereavement, school avoidance and emotional dysregulation. Two students also have identified ADHD, but the group is formed around current access needs rather than diagnosis.

## Starting evidence

Demand avoidance rises with public correction, unpredictable tasks and accumulated missed learning. Students can demonstrate more than written output alone suggests.

## Planned teaching response

Use predictable check-in and check-out, small achievable goals, low-arousal responses, private correction, explicit re-entry after absence and coordinated expectations across teachers. Maintain academic purpose; support is not a holding activity.

## Assessment and review loop

Engagement, attendance pattern, task completion, student voice and curriculum progress reviewed together; no single measure defines success.
