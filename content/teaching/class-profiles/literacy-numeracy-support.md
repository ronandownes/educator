> **Synthetic-data notice:** This class is entirely imaginary. The numbers and needs below are invented for planning and interview practice and do not describe real pupils.

## Class context

6 students receive three weekly support periods, largely while peers study Irish; every student in this fictional group has a documented Irish exemption.

## Strengths and interests

Practical problem solving, strong knowledge of local contexts and willingness to work in a predictable small-group routine.

## Fictional learning and access profile

4 students with dyslexia; 1 with developmental language disorder; 2 with ADHD; all require support with academic vocabulary, reading multi-step instructions and number confidence.

## Starting evidence

Errors often arise from interpreting the task, retaining steps or recording work rather than from the central concept alone.

## Planned teaching response

Integrate literacy and numeracy: morphology and vocabulary, read–represent–solve–check routines, visual organisers, calculators where appropriate, and direct transfer to current subject-class demands.

## Assessment and review loop

Curriculum-based measures, work samples from support and mainstream classes, student self-rating and six-week review with the support team.
